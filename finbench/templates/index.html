Each dot is one method with one model setting. Up is more accurate, left is cheaper (log scale). Hover a dot for details.

What this shows

  • Agentic search is the cheap, accurate cluster. With gpt-6-luna at high effort it ties PageIndex for the best accuracy (83%) at $0.0008 per question versus $0.0128, about 16× cheaper, with no index to build.
  • Vector RAG is the cheapest and refuses the most. About $0.0005 per question, but 13–20% of answers are "cannot find": the 10 closest chunks often miss the table the question needs.
  • PageIndex is not cheap per question. With Gemini it cost $0.12–0.31 per question, more than putting the whole filing in the prompt (~$0.10). Each turn of its tree search re-sends the conversation so far, and some searches run long (one question took 64 model calls in the pilot). The averages include those outliers.
  • Thinking helps cheap models cheaply. gpt-6-luna gains 7–17 points from no thinking to high effort, for a fraction of a cent.
  • Read the gaps with care. 30 questions, so one question moves accuracy by 3.3 points; differences under ~10 points are within noise. One judge model, strict grading against FinanceBench's gold answers, and each question asked against its own filing (finding the right filing among many is not tested).
